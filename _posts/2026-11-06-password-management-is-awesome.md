# password management is awesome and you should do it too!
urghhgrhgrhg how long has it been since i last posted here...? 2 MONTHS?!

yeah... sorry for the long hiatus, things has just been piling up and up and up and up and up and up and up and up and up and up and it is so frustrating

**BUT!** throughout my long hiatus, i have started managing my passwords!!! finally! after years of using the same password i conjured up when i was like, an infant<small>(lie)</small> or something, i finally, get this—manage my passwords! yeah, opsec just called, they said they're hiring me for $100m/year. all thanks to password management.

now, let's actually talk about the software, shall we? for my password management software, i use KeePass, as it is both not a pain in the ass to get going and works nicely with my syncthing setup. for my linux, android, and windows machines, i use KeePassXC, KeePassDX, and regular KeePass as the client respectively.

apart from the initial friction of resetting and making new passwords, <small>(which i lowkey really liked ngl)</small> it really is a joyful experience. i get the convenience of not needing to remember what email, password, or username i used for what service and the comfort of knowing that if one of the services i use gets a data leak, my other accounts don't get dragged down along with it too. it also scratches my brain using a good system **every. single. time.** i log into an account.

i heard bitwarden is also good but i haven't tried and i'm not planning to use it any time soon.

also coming up with a master password was fun :)
