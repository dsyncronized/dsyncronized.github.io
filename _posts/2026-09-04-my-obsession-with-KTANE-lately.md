# my latest obsession with defusing bombs
so i rediscovered KTaNE (keep talking and nobody explodes) three days ago, it had come to me when i was in search of a game that requires me to communicate under high pressure. and what do you know? KTaNE fills that KTaNE shaped hole in my head. how neat!!!

so ironically i started off playing solo, with my team comprising of me as the defuser and myself, i, and dsync as the experts. how cool, how amazing. but this was not even close to scratching the itch. so i sought out for communities of people to play and communicate with. miliseconds turn into seconds, seconds turn into minutes, and almost as if seeing an oasis in a desert, i found it. the KTaNE discord server of which i found from reddit.

there i saw a bunch of cool people who were very welcoming to an admittedly very bad defuser, but as i was going from bomb to bomb, i slowly got the hang of communicating efficiently as the defuser. and yes, i do know that this is a very new interest for me, as i have only played for three days and for less than 10 hours, but i feel like having the game centered around a community of people makes it so much more interesting in my opinion. as with many different people comes different methods of communicating to each other. making the game more varied than i thought despite not having too many modules in the vanilla game. (i myself have seen and solved every one of them ᗜ⩊ᗜ).

there isn't really much to the game other than talking and sometimes not exploding.<br>
what more to say other than communication?
